Kiseki Chart Widgets

Benchmark

Builds synthetic data of growing size, puts it in a chart the size of the box below, and times what the chart does per frame. The numbers are the main-thread time of one frame, from the start of the chart's frame to the end of drawing the series layer (the same figure as "draw … ms" in the demo's status bar), in milliseconds: median and 95th percentile over 90 real animation frames after a warm-up. Browsers round the clock to about 0.1 ms. A frame at 60 fps has 16.7 ms.

Zoomed out shows every point at once. Panning shows 150 bars and moves them a little every frame. Live tick changes the last bar and redraws, which includes updating the indicators. Load is setData plus the first frame. Bars carry volume and a 50-period EMA; ticks are drawn as a plain line.
